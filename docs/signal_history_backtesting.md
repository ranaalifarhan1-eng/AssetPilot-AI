# AssetPilot AI Signal History & Backtesting Specification

## Purpose

To ensure accountability and measure historical accuracy, every recommendation generated by AssetPilot AI is permanently recorded in the signal history log and evaluated at 1-day, 7-day, and 30-day intervals.

## Backtesting & Accuracy Metrics

The system will compute cumulative performance statistics over time:
- **Win Rate (%)**: Percentage of signals with positive returns at target horizon.
- **Average Return per Signal (%)**: Mean return across all historical ACCUMULATE / REDUCE recommendations.
- **Brier Score / Confidence Calibration**: Verifies whether high-confidence recommendations perform demonstrably better than low-confidence ones.
